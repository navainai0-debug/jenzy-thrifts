Quick WhatsApp messages

These buttons appear in every order. One tap opens WhatsApp with the message filled in for that customer. The words in curly brackets are replaced automatically:

{name}{order}{total}{items}{address}{city}{courier}{tracking}{tracking_link}{confirm_link}{review_link}{shop}

A message with {confirm_link} is the confirmation message: when the customer taps the link and presses Confirm, the order shows "Customer confirmed ✓".

Fake order protection

Every order gets a risk level — Low Medium High — from: past refused parcels, new customer, high order value, a phone that isn't a Pakistani mobile, the same phone on several accounts, many orders in a day, no house number, no map pin, and whether the customer confirmed on WhatsApp.

A parcel counts as refused when you mark the order Returned. Blocked numbers and accounts (Customers page) can never order.

Staff accounts
